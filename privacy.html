Privacy & terms

Last updated 9 September 2026.

Privacy

Your local progress and settings stay in your browser. If you sign in, we store your email address, account name, synced progress, and leaderboard scores so they can follow you between devices.

Email sign-in uses a one-time code. Google sign-in supplies your verified Google identity. We never receive your Google password. Optional account passwords are stored as secure hashes, not plaintext.

We do not sell personal information or build advertising profiles. Excerptle Pro payments are handled by Stripe; card details never reach Excerptle. To request account deletion or your data, email privacy@excerptle.io.

Terms

Book excerpts are public-domain works from Project Gutenberg. Excerptle is not affiliated with Project Gutenberg, Google, or Wordle.
