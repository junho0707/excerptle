Privacy & terms

Last updated 9 September 2026.

Privacy

Your local progress and settings stay in your browser. If you sign in, we store your email address, account name, synced progress, and leaderboard scores so they can follow you between devices.

Email sign-in uses a one-time code. Google sign-in supplies your verified Google identity. We never receive your Google password. Optional account passwords are stored as secure hashes, not plaintext.

Excerptle does not sell your personal information and does not build advertising profiles of its own. Advertising is served by Google, which is described below. Excerptle Pro payments are handled by Stripe; card details never reach Excerptle. To request account deletion or your data, email privacy@excerptle.io.

Cookies and local storage

Excerptle keeps your progress, streaks and settings in your browser’s local storage rather than on our servers, and sets a session cookie only if you sign in. Neither is used for advertising. Clearing site data clears them, and clears any progress that has not been synced to an account.

Advertising

Excerptle shows a single advertisement after a finished game, served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar device identifiers to serve those ads, to measure them, and — where you have allowed it — to personalise them based on your activity on this and other sites. That data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us. There are no ads beside a puzzle in progress, and none anywhere for Pro subscribers.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message appears before any advertising cookie is set, and you can accept, refuse, or choose which purposes you allow. Refusing is a single choice, and ads still appear — they are simply not personalised. You can change your answer at any time from the privacy link in that message.

Residents of US states with privacy laws, including California, can opt out of the sharing of personal information for personalised advertising through the same message. You can also review or switch off ad personalisation for your Google account at My Ad Center, and Excerptle Pro removes the advertisement entirely.
