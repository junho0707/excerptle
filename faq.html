Frequently asked questions

The rules themselves are on How to play. This page is everything people ask afterwards.

Is Excerptle free?

Yes, and all of it. No book, hint, mode or statistic sits behind a payment. The game is paid for by a single advertisement after a finished game, by tips, and by Excerptle Pro, which removes that advertisement for people who would rather pay than look at it.

Where do the books come from?

Every text comes from Project Gutenberg, the archive of works whose copyright has expired. That makes the canon here mostly pre-1930 — Austen, Melville, the Brontës, Conrad, Twain, Wells, and a long tail of adventure serials that were enormous in their day and are forgotten now. At most one chapter of any book is hosted here; for the rest, go to Gutenberg, where it is free. Excerptle is not affiliated with Project Gutenberg.

How is the daily puzzle chosen, and when does it change?

Everyone in the world gets the same book on the same day, from a running order fixed in advance, and it rolls over at midnight Pacific time. Nobody is quietly served an easier puzzle than anyone else.

Do I have to spell the title exactly right?

No. Close spelling is accepted — “Pride and Predjudice” is fine — and a leading “The” is optional. What does not work is one vague word: a guess like “great” will neither solve the book nor earn you a title-word nudge.

Does a wrong guess reveal more of the book?

No. A wrong guess costs one of your six and reveals nothing. Hints reveal the opening excerpt, then genre, year, setting, and author in order.

Do I need an account?

No, and the game is complete without one. Progress, streaks and settings are stored in your own browser. Signing in exists so those numbers can follow you to another device and so you can appear on the leaderboard.

What does Excerptle Pro do?

It removes the single post-game advertisement. That is its only privilege — it unlocks no books, hints or modes, and it never will. Payments run through Stripe, so card details never reach Excerptle, and you can cancel any time from the billing portal inside the game. Current prices are shown when you open Pro.

How does Battle mode work?

Battle connects two browsers and hands both players the same book at the same moment. First correct title wins.

I lost my streak. Can it be restored?

If you were signed out, your statistics lived only in that browser’s storage, so clearing site data, private browsing or a new device loses them for good — there is no copy anywhere else to restore from. Signing in syncs everything from that point on.

How do I report a wrong answer or a broken puzzle?

Email the puzzle number and what went wrong to privacy@excerptle.io. Gutenberg publishes some works several times over under different numbers, so a duplicate or an odd title is the likeliest bug, and those are worth telling us about.

Who makes Excerptle?

Provable Learning LLC, an independent education studio. More on the about page.
