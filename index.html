    Privacy

    What stays on your device. Your progress, guesses, hint counts, scores and settings are kept in your browser’s localStorage. Clearing site data erases them. Nothing here is sent anywhere unless you sign in.

    If you sign in. Signing in with Google gives us the name and email address in your Google account, so your streaks and scores can follow you between devices. Signing in with email gives us that address and a sign-in code or password. We do not receive your Google password.

    What we don’t do. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use it to build advertising profiles.

    Ads. A single ad slot sits on the post-game screen, and there are no ads anywhere else in the game. It is filled by Google AdSense, which may set cookies or read a device identifier to choose and measure ads; Google’s use of that data is described in its partner sites policy. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change that answer at any time from the consent link in the ad. Google’s script is loaded on every page of the site so the slot can be filled when you reach it. With Excerptle Pro, no ad is ever requested or shown.

    Payments. Excerptle Pro is billed by Stripe. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us or your browser’s storage — we keep only a customer reference and whether your subscription is active. Tips go through Ko-fi, which is entirely off this site.

    Book texts. Excerpts are public-domain works from Project Gutenberg, served as static files from this site. Reading a puzzle sends nothing to Gutenberg.

    Battles. Battle mode connects the two browsers directly (WebRTC, via PeerJS for signalling). Your display name, guesses and hint count go to your opponent for the length of the match, and are not stored on our side.

    Your choices. To delete a signed-in account and its scores, or to ask what we hold, email privacy@excerptle.io. To wipe local data, clear site data for excerptle.io in your browser.
